# ELV — AI-authored directional test specification

Written for this rebuild before executing its new tests. This is an AI software-test specification, not a human AI-off prediction or historical precommit. No original student evidence is asserted.

1. Shift every annual blended gross margin up 0.50 percentage points with all other independent controls fixed: expect higher FY2030 operating income, FCFE and value. Lower benefits also reduce claims funding, partly offsetting the gain.
2. Raise cost of equity from 10% to 11%: expect identical statements and lower value.
3. Raise debt interest by one percentage point: expect lower earnings, FCFE and value because this is an equity cash-flow model.
4. Increase claims timing by one day: expect no operating-profit change, higher near-term CFO/FCFE, and a higher claims liability. This is a payment-timing sensitivity, not free economic profit.
5. Corrupt FY2026 closing cash by minus $100m: expect both balance-sheet and cash-flow failures and withheld valuation.
6. Request excessive distributions beyond the cash floor and revolver capacity: expect a visible liquidity failure and withheld valuation.
7. Include negative interim equity cash flows with their signs; do not omit funding needs. Reject an invalid terminal boundary.
